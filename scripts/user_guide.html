1 Authentication & Multi-Tenant Setup

Securely access your workspace using Google OAuth or join an existing team organization via an encrypted invite code.

https://localhost:3000/login

🔑 Google OAuth Sign-In

Instant

Click "Sign In with Google" to authenticate via Supabase Auth. Your personal workspace is initialized automatically with Row Level Security (RLS).

🏢 Join via 6-Digit Code

Collaborate

Invited by a teammate? Enter the 6-character Workspace Code in the login card to immediately bind your account to their organization.

✨ Switching & Creating Workspaces
Click the Workspace Switcher in the top navigation bar. Owners can create new organization workspaces, copy 6-digit invite codes, or invite members by per-person link.
🛡️ Role-Based Safety Protections
Invited teammates are locked from accidentally deleting workspaces or leaving without admin consent, maintaining uninterrupted enterprise compliance.
💡
Pro-Tip for Workspace Owners: You can create multiple independent client workspaces (e.g. GLS Nexus, Acme Corp) under one login and seamlessly switch between them without logging out.

2 Live Audio Recording & File Ingestion

Capture conversations live in real-time or upload pre-recorded meetings across multiple languages.

https://localhost:3000/new-meeting
🎙️

1. Live In-Browser

Click "Start Live Recording". Echoes utilizes real-time audio chunking, volume meter visualization, and pauses.

📁

2. Audio File Drop

Upload .mp3, .wav, .m4a, or .webm. Automatically compressed in-browser before upload to conserve bandwidth.

🌐

3. Multi-Lingual

Select English, Hindi, or Gujarati. Transcribes native phrasing and translates action items automatically.

⚙️ Audio Pipeline Architecture
When recording stops, Echoes uploads the encrypted audio directly to your dedicated Supabase Storage bucket, triggering the AssemblyAI diarization webhook and Gemini extraction pipeline.
⚡
Audio Safety Shield: If your network disconnects during a live recording, Echoes stores local cached audio chunks in browser IndexedDB to prevent meeting audio loss.

4 Kanban Task Board & Integrations

Convert conversation promises into completed deliverables with real-time velocity tracking and 1-click sync.

https://localhost:3000/tasks
📌 Drag & Drop Kanban Lifecycle
Organize extracted items into To Do, In Progress, Review, and Completed. Tasks update in Supabase with instant optimistic UI feedback.
📅 1-Click Google Calendar Sync
Click "Add to Calendar" to schedule follow-up deliverables directly on Google Calendar with meeting deep-links included.

Dispatch formatted meeting digests and personalized action lists to all attendees via Nodemailer (Gmail SMTP & Resend).

Generate pixel-perfect executive PDF summaries or editable Microsoft Word (.docx) reports ready for distribution.
🚀
Meeting Velocity Metric: The Task Board aggregates velocity scores across all team members, tracking how fast tasks move from "To Do" to "Completed" each sprint.

5 Restricted Member Portal & Governance

Enterprise data isolation, default "Assigned Items Only" scoping, and address-bar route protection.

https://localhost:3000/ (Restricted Member Portal View)

🔒 Default "Assigned Items Only"

Strict Default

Every newly invited team member automatically defaults to restricted data scope. They can only see tasks and meetings explicitly assigned to them.

🚫 Address Bar URL Guard

Guarded

If a restricted teammate attempts to manually type /meetings or /team in their browser, they are instantly redirected to their clean Member Portal.

👥 Workspace Owner Administrative Controls

⚡ Fluid Scope Switch: Toggle any member between "Full Workspace Access" and "Assigned Items Only" in 1 click on the /team page.
🛡️ Revoke Access Safe Retention: Revoking access instantly removes workspace login tokens while preserving historical task assignments in your directory.
